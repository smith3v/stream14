# stream14

stream14 is a native iPhone app for recording and broadcasting live events. It
combines camera video and microphone audio with an optional web overlay and saves
an MP4 recording on your iPhone. The app is currently distributed through TestFlight.

You can record locally without a Google account. Connect YouTube to see your
channel and select an existing scheduled encoder event. When you confirm Go Live,
stream14 prepares the event and sends the composed video and audio directly to
YouTube. Connecting an account or selecting an event leaves camera upload idle.
